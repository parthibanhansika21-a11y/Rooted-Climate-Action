🌍 Environmental Impacts

🌡️

Climate Change

Livestock produce 14.5% of global greenhouse gases. Cows emit methane — 25× more potent than CO₂. A single cow produces 70-120kg of methane yearly.

🌳

Deforestation

91% of Amazon deforestation is for cattle ranching or soy feed. An area the size of a football pitch is cleared every second.

💧

Water Pollution

Factory farms produce 500 million tons of manure yearly in the US alone. Runoff creates dead zones in oceans and contaminates rivers.

💊

Antibiotic Resistance

73% of all antibiotics go to livestock, not humans. This fuels superbugs. By 2050, resistant infections could kill 10M people/year.

🐔

Animal Welfare

80% of pigs have tail bites from stress. Battery hens live in spaces smaller than an A4 paper. Broiler chickens grow so fast their legs break.

🦋

Biodiversity Loss

Meat production is the #1 cause of species extinction. 60% of wildlife has been lost since 1970, largely due to habitat destruction for farmland.

E-Waste & Recycling

“Singapore generates 60,000 tonnes of e-waste yearly. Let's learn what goes where!”
— Nibbles the Caterpillar

Look deep into nature, and then you will understand everything better.

— Albert Einstein

❌ General Waste Bin (NOT recyclable)

  • 🍕 Food waste & leftovers
  • 🥤 Used tissues & napkins
  • 🪣 Ceramic & broken glass
  • 👶 Diapers & sanitary items
  • 🛍️ Plastic bags with food residue
  • 💡 Light bulbs (go to e-waste)
  • 🔋 Batteries (go to e-waste!)
  • 💊 Expired medicines

✅ Blue Recycling Bin

  • 🥤 Rinsed plastic bottles & containers
  • 📰 Newspapers, paper, cardboard
  • 🥫 Metal cans (rinsed)
  • 🍾 Glass bottles & jars (rinsed)
  • 👕 Clean old clothing & textiles
  • 📦 Flattened carton boxes
  • 🧴 Empty toiletry bottles (rinsed)
  • ♻️ Items marked with recycling symbols

💡 Tip: Rinse containers before recycling. Food contamination can ruin an entire batch. When in doubt, throw it out — wishful recycling does more harm than good.
